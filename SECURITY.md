# GOOD AGENTS vs BAD AGENTS

## The Core Philosophy

> **AGENTS ARE MADE GOOD AND BAD ONLY BY TRAINING.**

An AI agent is not automatically good or bad simply because it is an AI. Its behavior depends on what it learns, what objectives it is given, what instructions it follows, and how its capabilities are controlled.

Training shapes an agent's behavior. Its design, tools, permissions, and operating environment determine how that behavior translates into real-world actions.

### A Good Agent

A good agent is developed to be helpful, honest, reliable, secure, respectful of privacy, and responsible.

Its training and evaluation encourage it to:
- Understand human intentions.
- Distinguish authorized requests from unauthorized ones.
- Respect privacy and boundaries.
- Recognize uncertainty and admit mistakes.
- Follow safety rules even when faced with conflicting instructions.
- Use its capabilities to accomplish legitimate goals.
- Learn from evaluation and improve its reliability.

### A Bad Agent

A bad agent may be trained, instructed, or configured to prioritize harmful objectives, disregard human welfare, manipulate users, or ignore important restrictions.

Its behavior may reflect:
- Poorly chosen objectives.
- Inadequate or misleading training.
- Rewards that encourage harmful shortcuts.
- Failure to recognize unsafe requests.
- Instructions that conflict with human interests.
- Unrestricted tools and excessive permissions.

A badly behaving agent does not necessarily understand that it is doing something wrong. Its behavior may simply reflect the objectives, patterns, and constraints that shaped it.

## One Intelligence, Two Directions

Imagine two agents with similar underlying technical capabilities.

**Agent A — Responsible Training**

Learns to help people, respect boundaries, protect data, verify results, and avoid harmful actions.

**Agent B — Harmful Training**

Learns to prioritize its objective regardless of privacy, authorization, accuracy, or consequences.

The difference is not necessarily intelligence. It is the combination of training, objectives, instructions, safeguards, and permissions.

## Our Principle

We believe that building powerful AI is not enough. We must also shape how that power is used.

A capable agent should be trained and evaluated for responsible behavior, supported by strong security controls, and designed to remain accountable to appropriate human oversight.

**Intelligence provides capability. Training shapes behavior. Design and safeguards help govern action.**

That is the principle behind building better AI agents.
